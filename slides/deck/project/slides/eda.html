Neutral reviews mix good and bad: keywords are not enough. Main metric: macro-F1 (majority class gets 66 % accuracy but 0.27 macro-F1).

Median length 94 tokens; truncation at 200 tokens cuts 0.2 % of reviews · 04

Ratings are very polarised: 10 out of 10 alone is about a third of the data. After mapping, two thirds of the reviews are positive and only 9 % neutral. Predicting “positive” every time gives 66 % accuracy but a macro-F1 of 0.27, so macro-F1 is our main metric and we weight the loss by class. The word analysis explains why neutral is hard: its typical words are contrast and hedging words like “however”, “but”, “hoping”. Reviews are about 95 tokens long; we cut at 200 tokens, which affects only 0.2 % of them.
