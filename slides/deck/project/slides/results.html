H = 256 (LSTMP: P = 128) · mean ± std over 3 seeds · test accuracy 78.7–79.8 % for all cells · 07

This is our main comparison: same width, same protocol, three seeds each. All four cells land within about half a point of macro-F1, so the cell is not a game changer on this task. But the ranking is consistent: WMC and CIFG are about half a point better than vanilla on test and one point better on validation. On validation the gap is significant; on test, with only three seeds, the p-value is 0.13, so we call it a consistent trend, not a proof. The clearer difference is speed of learning: CIFG and WMC reach 62 % validation macro-F1 after 3 to 4 epochs, vanilla needs more than 5. CIFG is our preferred cell: same score as WMC, with a quarter fewer parameters than vanilla and half as many as WMC. The neutral class stays hard for every model: F1 around 30 %.
