CIFG, H = 256 per direction, Xavier init, Adam lr 2e-3, batch 128 · chosen on validation, test evaluated once · 10

For the final model we combined what we learned: the CIFG cell, Xavier initialisation, a learning rate of 2e-3, and we let the model read the review in both directions. We trained two candidates, one and two layers, and chose on validation only: the two-layer model wins, and we evaluated it once on the test set. It reaches 65.6 % macro-F1 and 80.3 % accuracy, 1.6 points above the vanilla baseline. It also converges very fast: its best validation score is at epoch 4. The confusion matrix shows where the errors are: positive and negative reviews are mostly right, but only 36 % of neutral reviews are recognised; the rest are split between negative and positive, and some clearly polar reviews are pulled to neutral by the class weights.
