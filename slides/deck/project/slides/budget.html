At equal size, structure beats capacity

At 296k: WMC +0.9 F1 (p = 0.02) and CIFG +0.7 (p = 0.03) over vanilla. Shrinking WMC costs nothing; growing vanilla gains nothing. LSTMP works best at P = H/2.

Vanilla re-trained with H = 215 and WMC with H = 172 to match CIFG and LSTMP (≈ 296k recurrent parameters) · 08

A fair objection to the previous slide: the cells do not have the same number of parameters. So we re-trained the vanilla cell with a hidden size of 215 and WMC with 172, so that all four have about 296 thousand recurrent parameters. At equal budget the gap is clearer and now statistically significant: WMC gains 0.9 points and CIFG 0.7 points over vanilla. Notice that WMC shrunk to half its size loses nothing, and the vanilla cell made bigger gains almost nothing. So the improvement comes from how the cell is built, not from how big it is. On the right, the LSTMP projection size: compressing too much, to 32, costs about 1.6 points; no compression at all is not better either. Half the hidden size is the sweet spot, on a single seed.
