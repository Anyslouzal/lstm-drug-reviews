Goal of the task

Read a patient’s review, predict its sentiment

positive · 10/10

“Added this med and pain is finally under control. Thank you so much.”

neutral · 6/10

“Really helps with mood swings and made me calmer. It also made me gain 50lbs in 6 months.”

negative · 1/10

“Tried it a few times but it gave me horrible, disturbing nightmares each time.”

1

Baseline: a vanilla LSTM written with nn.Linear only

2

Three non-vanilla cells: WMC, CIFG and LSTMP (one more than required)

3

Hyper-parameters: learning rate, optimizer, initialisation, batch size

Ratings 1–4 → negative · 5–6 → neutral · 7–10 → positive (Gräßer et al., 2018) · 02

The dataset is the UCI Drugs.com review dataset: free-text patient reviews with a 1 to 10 rating. Following the dataset authors, we turn the rating into three sentiment classes. Look at the neutral example: it says something good and something bad. That is what makes the task interesting for a recurrent model, which has to weigh the whole review. Our three goals: a vanilla baseline, three other LSTM cells from the appendix, and a hyper-parameter study.
